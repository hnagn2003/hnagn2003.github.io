I am an AI Research Resident at Qualcomm AI Research (Hanoi, Vietnam), supervised by Dr. Anh Tran and Assoc. Prof. Cuong Pham. I hold a B.S. in Information Technology from VNU University of Engineering and Technology (VNU-UET), Vietnam National University, Hanoi (2021–2025).

My research focuses on efficient generative modeling — particularly one-step diffusion distillation, controllable generation, and pixel- and latent-space generative models. Since August 2026, I have also been working on agentic memory systems for wearable AI, focusing on multimodal RAG, long-term memory, and memory retrieval for on-device/on-glass agents, supervised by Dat Quoc Nguyen.
