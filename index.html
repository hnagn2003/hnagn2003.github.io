👋 Hi! I'm Ngan. Nice to meet y'all! I am an AI Research Resident at Qualcomm AI Research (Hanoi, Vietnam), supervised by Dr. Anh Tran and Assoc. Prof. Cuong Pham. I hold a B.S. in Information Technology from VNU University of Engineering and Technology (VNU-UET), Vietnam National University, Hanoi (2021–2025).

Research Interests

My current research focuses on efficient generative modeling, particularly one-step diffusion distillation, controllable image generation, and pixel- and latent-space diffusion. I am also interested in exploring vision-language models (VLMs), agentic AI, video generation, and world models in future research.

FlashControl: One-Step Controllable Generation via Distillation-Friendly Single-Stream Teachers
Ngan Nguyen*, Dung Nguyen*, Quan Dao, Dimitris N. Metaxas, Minh Hoai, Cuong Pham, Anh Tuan Tran
Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2026
We make controllable image generation possible in just one step. Instead of using complicated control branches like ControlNet, we build a simpler teacher model and distill it into a fast, one-step generator.
arXiv
Cross-Space Distillation: Teaching One-Step Students with Modern Diffusion Teachers
Ngan Nguyen*, Anh Nguyen*, Duc Vu*, Trung Dao, Viet Nguyen, Quan Dao, Kien Nguyen, Chi Tran, Phong Nguyen, Khoi Nguyen, Cuong Pham, Dimitris N. Metaxas, Vishal M. Patel, Anh Tran
European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV), 2026
We enable large, powerful diffusion models to teach smaller models, even when they use different latent spaces. We introduce a lightweight bridge between them, improving image quality while keeping generation fast and requiring only one step.
arXiv
InverFill: One-Step Inversion for Enhanced Few-Step Diffusion Inpainting
Duc Vu*, Ngan Nguyen*, Kien Nguyen*, Trong-Tung Nguyen*, Phong Nguyen, Khoi Nguyen, Cuong Pham, Anh Tran
IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR), 2026
We make image inpainting faster and more natural. Instead of starting from random noise, we predict noise that already contains information from the original image, helping few-step diffusion models fill missing regions more seamlessly without retraining a specialized inpainting model.
Project Page arXiv
Discrete Noise Inversion for Next-scale Autoregressive Text-based Image Editing (VARIN)
Quan Dao, Xiaoxiao He, Ligong Han, Ngan Nguyen, Amin Heyrani Nobar, Faez Ahmed, Han Zhang, Viet Anh Nguyen, Dimitris N. Metaxas
European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV), 2026
We enable text-guided image editing with autoregressive image models without additional training. By reversing the model's discrete sampling process, we can reconstruct an image and edit specific parts while preserving its original structure and background.
arXiv

Beyond the VAE Bottleneck: One-Step Pixel Diffusion from Latent Teachers
Ngan Nguyen*, Duc Vu*, Anh Nguyen, Cuong Pham, Anh Tran
Under Review, 2026
We make one-step image generation faster by generating pixels directly, removing the need for a VAE decoder. We transfer knowledge from powerful latent diffusion models into a pixel-space generator, achieving high-quality images with at least 1.5× faster inference than the compared one-step latent models.

VideoDrift: Plug-and-Play Video Refinement for Diffusion Models via KV-Anchored Attention
Ngan Nguyen, Duc Vu, Trong-Tung Nguyen, Phuc Hong Lai, Cuong Pham, Anh Tran
Under Review, 2026
We improve the visual quality of generated videos without rerunning the original video model. We invert and regenerate each frame using a strong one-step image model, then use attention from reference frames to maintain visual consistency across the video.
